How the calculations work

Calculation notes

The sign on date and time are used exactly as entered, including for future planning. The countdown is relative to the current local time. Schedule times use the selected sign-on time zone; the live clock uses your device's local time zone. Your selections are remembered on this device. Past sign-on dates advance to today when reopening.

Hotel transport times can be saved and updated. This prototype saves them in the browser's local storage; downloaded-file previews may not retain settings. A hosted version can make saving more reliable across sessions and devices.
