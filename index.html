iPhone alarm setup (one time)

Create a shortcut named exactly Leave for Work Alarm in Apple's Shortcuts app.

  1. Add Get Dates from Input. Set its input to Shortcut Input.
  2. Add Create Alarm. Set the time to the Dates result, name it Leave for Work, and leave Repeat off.

Then tap Set alarm beside a calculated time. This opens Shortcuts; verify the created alarm in Clock. Changing your plan does not update alarms already created. Clock alarms ring at the next occurrence of their time, so buttons only support the next occurrence, not dates further ahead.

Calculation notes

The sign on date and time are used exactly as entered, including for future planning. The countdown is relative to the current local time. Times use your device's local time zone. Your selections are remembered on this device. Past sign-on dates advance to today when reopening.

Hotel transport times can be saved and updated. This prototype saves them in the browser's local storage; downloaded-file previews may not retain settings. A hosted version can make saving more reliable across sessions and devices.
